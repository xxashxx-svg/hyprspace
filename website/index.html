Your subscription

Your CLI, your quota, no middleman.

HyprSpace never touches your API keys and never resells your usage. Each pane just runs the agent you already signed into. Log in once, then run them all at once.

~/projects
# sign in to the agents you already pay for
$ claude      # Claude Code
$ gemini      # Gemini CLI
$ codex login # OpenAI Codex

# then open HyprSpace and tile them. that's it.
No keys stored
Your token stays in your CLI. HyprSpace only reads display fields like your plan.
No quota resold
You get exactly what your plan gives you. HyprSpace is the workspace, not a reseller.
Resume any pane
Every Claude pane keeps its own chat, so reopening it picks up right where you left off.
